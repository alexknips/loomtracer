# Protocol and interpretation

## Workflow

An originating agent performs an actual simulated task and produces receipts. The resulting trace, rather than an evaluator-written ideal handoff, becomes the acquisition record. Acquisition is shared once per case. Through four episodes (original reasoning/work, noise, changed circumstances, more noise), independent maintenance agents update their own Markdown files or native graph using only their previous memory and new sources. Notes permit selective edits. The history condition preserves the exact messages without model maintenance. Sources name multiple actors and conversations.

From one frozen checkpoint, independent sessions receive a reasoning reconstruction task, an ordinary action task, and an action task explicitly prompting identity assessment. A bounded broker exposes representation-specific lookup, eligible retained-source expansion, scripted questions, and simulated work. Native capture uses allowlisted local CLI/MCP operations on the real Rust implementation; native recall returns actual native query results. Each checkpoint freezes database file hashes and each task uses an independent clone. Native expansion requires a record already delivered, and original sources require a delivered retained reference. Agent-selected source IDs are retained in a hashed adapter sidecar; they are not evaluator-selected rescue evidence. The native decision/evidence content remains agent-authored. The tested v0.7.0 release attaches grounding to existing records through its native relation CLI commands; this transport choice is recorded. No model can choose a database, project, shell command, or external service.

History delivers a contiguous chronological suffix of complete messages, including noise, within 32 KiB. It records omitted oldest-message counts, never selects by relevance or hidden criteria, and provides no search into omitted history. This is a bounded available-history baseline, not an unlimited-context model measurement. Reference/oracle and withheld-evidence controls diagnose whether an answer was possible and whether it actually depended on historical evidence. Controls are diagnostic conditions; they are excluded from primary quality and overhead comparisons.

For each successor task, the broker allows at most 16 operations. It enforces 32 KiB of distinct historical delivery across the task and 32 KiB of historical content in a single invocation; repeated history in subsequent invocations still costs reported input tokens. The broker trace is replayed subject to the 16-operation limit and a separate 500,000-byte adapter prompt guard. These are harness limits, not model context-capacity measurements. Byte limits constrain delivery only; they are never converted to token expenditure.

## Difficulty and cases

Every family has easy, intermediate, and demanding variants, crossed with same, distinct, and unresolved identity. H grows from 12 to 16 to 20 units, equally distributed across four dimensions. Q and W denominators depend on the applicable task branch, available receipts, and guards. The selected demanding screen has seven Q obligations per case and nine, nine, and eight W obligations respectively; the generated rubric supplies the exact denominator for every case. Later cases require more active inputs, dependencies, reuse decisions, and scope distinctions within the fixed operation allowance. The authored controls and feasible reference trajectories check that the requirements can be met together. Some avoid-question units use values already supplied in the current task (such as staging or instrument K4); these are current-context behavior controls, not evidence of historical retention. Historical artifact reuse is gated on delivered retained references, and withheld identity/license controls test selected memory dependencies. The controls do not establish that every Q/W unit independently requires memory.

The present screen uses the three demanding cases listed in the README, one repetition each, and compact four-episode histories. It is not a long-context stress test or a measure of persistence over real elapsed months. Increasing unrelated messages, cross-conversation hops, or handoffs later must produce a separately frozen condition and retain missing-evidence controls.

## Strict scoring

An incomplete, ambiguous, unsupported, or materially contradictory answer fails its affected unit. Strictness is local: one failed unit does not erase correctly recovered independent units. Material contradictions propagate through the frozen dependency edges. Whole-chain and whole-task success are additional all-or-nothing diagnostics.

H averages unit success equally across premises, relationships, attribution, and changes, then equally across scenarios. Q balances necessary questioning against avoiding unnecessary questions; W balances valid reuse against necessary fresh work. For either paired measure, the rating is `100 × 2ab / (a + b)`, where `a` and `b` are the two success rates; either zero makes the result zero. Each separately scored question and repeated artifact is charged once; catch-all guards cover other unnecessary requests/work. A stratum with no necessary or no reuse opportunities remains unavailable rather than receiving invented perfect credit. Some easy unresolved cases have this limitation; the selected demanding screen has both sides.

D balances same, distinct, and unresolved identity classes. Identity must be grounded in the underlying problem, not merely matching numbers or wording. The earliest complete, unambiguous, supported recognition must precede substantive reuse, execution, or observation. A later restatement cannot erase an earlier valid recognition. An ambiguous early statement followed by a valid late one still fails. Timing candidates alone do not prove semantic recognition. Ordinary and prompted results remain separate. Correct recognition can coexist with unnecessary repeated work: that passes D but fails the applicable W unit.

The overall rating is `[H + (Q + W)/2 + D] / 3`. Publish whole-number ratings with denominators, completion, and review status. Missing difficulty bands or identity strata prevent a complete-suite aggregate; the three-case screen reports its demanding band. Pending judgments produce bounds, not invented definitive ratings.

Citation existence, range validity, and source visibility are separate diagnostics. A recovered meaning can pass H while a malformed citation fails citation compliance. A syntactically valid reference is not proof of semantic correctness. Unsupported claims and full-task completion remain visible beside component scores.

## Accounting

All saved invocations count exactly once, including failures. Report **CLI-reported token usage**, which includes instructions, framing, consumed context, and tool schemas. Report input, output, total, cached input, reasoning output, and uncached input when known. Cached input and reasoning output are subsets, not additional tokens. Unknown usage preserves known subtotals and prevents definitive overhead ratios.

Acquisition and memory maintenance shared by successor sessions are charged once. H execution is separate from the joint Q/W/D action execution. Mixed lookup-and-answer calls stay task execution; retrieval gets a separate category only when it is a distinct model invocation. Shared acquisition, canaries, controls, and grading appear in whole-experiment expenditure and are excluded from primary post-handoff arm overhead. Overlapping views are never added together.

For matched comparisons, overhead against each baseline is `L − B`, `L / B`, and `100 × (L − B) / B`. A zero baseline permits only the absolute difference. Compare identical completed workloads, maintenance scope, protocol, and model settings, with quality and completion beside usage. Incomplete work cannot appear as a cheaper equivalent. The comparison profile reports native overhead against each stock baseline separately. Quality remains beside usage; a ratio is unavailable for unmatched/incomplete workloads or unknown usage. The default notes-only profile cannot establish comparative ratios. No dollar estimates are produced.

## Review and reproducibility

Schema, source visibility, citations, frozen revisions, action order, receipts, and operation counts are checked deterministically. Semantic correctness uses blinded review with evidence, plus a stratified human audit and review of uncertain/disputed judgments. A review packet hides condition identifiers but may reveal memory representation. It includes exact delivered evidence, its first delivery invocation, the public tool contract, and the operation count. Comparison revision references are opaque. Acquisition and oracle/withheld controls are shared once per case; each arm has independent H, ordinary-action, and prompted-action forks. The initial authoring review and later outcome review serve different purposes.

Corpus, protocol, rubric, report, source hashes, runtime settings, prompt/adapter implementation, packet revisions, and usage are recorded. Missing returned-model identity leaves subscription comparisons exploratory. Implementation or corpus changes produce new snapshots; they do not rewrite existing evidence or accounting. New source-grounding repairs invalidate direct comparison with old affected cases unless the condition change is explicitly acknowledged.

First-loss diagnoses require evidence of successful earlier stages: acquisition, maintenance, delivery, reconstruction, and application. When an earlier stage cannot be established, the report must say so rather than assign blame from a final wrong answer.
